At NEXGEN TIMES, available at NEXGEN TIMES Media, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by NEXGEN TIMES and how we use it.

Google is one of a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

NEXGEN TIMES follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files include IP addresses, browser type, ISP, date/time stamp, referring/exit pages.
