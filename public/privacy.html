Privacy Policy & Reader Data Governance Charter

At Prime Media Publishing Group (https://primemedia.site), safeguarding the digital privacy, anonymity, and statutory data rights of our global readership is a foundational pillar of our journalistic mission. This Privacy Policy & Data Governance Charter explains what technical telemetry we collect, how we process reader interactions, our strict data minimization standards, and how readers can exercise their statutory rights under international privacy frameworks.

1. Scope of Data Collection & Data Minimization Principles

Prime Media operates as an open-access digital news publication. Readers are never required to create an account, submit government identification, or provide financial details to read our investigative dispatches, market analysis, or technology guides. We adhere strictly to the principle of Data Minimization: we only process technical telemetry necessary to deliver low-latency web pages, prevent automated distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) abuse, measure aggregate readership trends, and fund free journalism through compliant programmatic advertising.

  • Voluntary Correspondence Data: When you voluntarily subscribe to the Prime Media Daily Briefing newsletter or submit a press inquiry or confidential news tip via our Newsroom Contact Desk, we collect the email address and message content you explicitly provide.
  • Automated Server Log Telemetry: Like all enterprise web infrastructure, our edge servers record standard HTTP request headers, including truncated or anonymized Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser user-agent strings, operating system classification, referring URLs, request timestamps, and country-level geolocation headers (such as Cloudflare/edge country codes).
  • Confidential Whistleblower Protection: Editorial tips submitted to our investigative desk (see our Editorial Masthead) are segregated from commercial analytics databases and are never shared with third-party marketing vendors.

2. Cookie Inventory, Google Analytics 4 (GA4) & Advertising Telemetry

Prime Media uses first-party functional storage and carefully vetted third-party measurement and advertising partners to sustain our newsroom operations. Below is our transparent inventory of cookies and browser storage mechanisms active on primemedia.site:

Provider / Mechanism Category Technical Purpose & Retention
SessionStorage (homepage_page) Strictly Necessary Remembers your current pagination state when navigating back from an article. Cleared automatically when browser tab closes.
Google Analytics 4 (_ga, _ga_*) Aggregate Analytics Measures anonymous article readership, scroll engagement, and device performance metrics. IP addresses are anonymized by default in GA4 (Retention: up to 14 months).
Google AdSense / DoubleClick (IDE, __gads) Programmatic Advertising Enables frequency capping, fraud prevention, and contextual or personalized ad delivery across the Google Display Network.

3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & AdSense Program Disclosures

Google LLC acts as an authorized third-party advertising vendor on Prime Media (Publisher ID: pub-9492642167600744, verified via our official /ads.txt ledger). Google uses cookies, including the DoubleClick DART cookie and Privacy Sandbox Topics API signals, to serve advertisements to visitors based upon their visit to primemedia.site and other sites across the internet.

  • Opting Out of Personalized Advertising: Readers may decline the use of personalized advertising cookies at any time by visiting Google's Ad Settings portal at https://myadcenter.google.com/ or the Google Advertising Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • Industry-Wide Ad Choices: You may also opt out of third-party vendor cookies participating in the Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA) or Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) via aboutads.info or youronlinechoices.eu (for European Economic Area residents).

4. Statutory Rights Under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA & India DPDP Act

Depending on your jurisdiction of residence, you hold specific enforceable statutory rights regarding your personal data:

  • European Economic Area (EEA), UK & Switzerland (GDPR): Under Articles 15–22 of the General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to request access to personal data, rectification of inaccurate records, erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"), restriction of processing, data portability, and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • California & U.S. State Privacy Laws (CCPA / CPRA / VCDPA): California residents hold the right to know what categories of personal information are collected, the right to request deletion of personal information, the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights. Prime Media does not sell reader email lists or personal identity records to data brokers.
  • Republic of India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — DPDP Act): Data Principals in India hold the right to obtain a summary of personal data processed, the right to correction and erasure of personal data, the right of grievance redressal, and the right to nominate another individual to exercise rights in the event of incapacity.

5. Information Security, Encryption & Data Retention Schedule

All traffic between your browser and primemedia.site is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS) with strict HSTS enforcement. Aggregate server access logs are rotated and purged within 30 days unless required to investigate active cybersecurity incidents. Newsletter subscriber records are retained until the subscriber clicks "Unsubscribe" or requests deletion via our Data Protection Desk.

6. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

Prime Media produces analytical journalism intended for general adult and professional audiences. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in applicable European jurisdictions). If a parent or guardian believes a minor has submitted personal information to our newsroom, please contact us immediately for permanent removal.
